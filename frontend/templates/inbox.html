    Real-time

    Phishing Guidelines

    Know the signs. Phishy scans automatically, but awareness is your first line of defense.

    Suspicious Links

    Hover before clicking. If the URL uses a raw IP or a look-alike domain (e.g. paypa1.com), don't click.

    Sender Spoofing

    Check the actual email domain, not just the display name. "Google Support" can be sent from scam@random.ru.

    Urgency Tactics

    "Account suspended in 24 hours" is a pressure tactic. Legitimate companies don't rush you into clicking links.

    Credential Requests

    No legitimate service asks for your password by email. Go directly to the website — never through an email link.

    Dangerous Attachments

    Never open .exe, .bat, .scr, or double-extension files like invoice.pdf.exe.

    Pro Tip: Phishy scores emails 0–100. Any score above 50 warrants caution. High-risk emails should never be opened without verifying the sender through a separate channel.
